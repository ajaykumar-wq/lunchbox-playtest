How to play

  1. Tap the top item of a stack. It flies to a lunchbox that needs it, or waits on the bench.
  2. Fill the boxes before the 5-slot bench overflows. Undo and +1 slot help when you're stuck.
  3. Try the Skin button, play as far as you like, then send your thoughts below.
